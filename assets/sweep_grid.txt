ObsCal 7-point excitation grid and per-band role
================================================
Each frequency sits where a distinct equivalent-circuit element dominates the
spectrum, so the grid is deliberately uneven rather than logarithmically spaced.
The single-frequency comparator channel is 12.5 Hz and is reported as
transepithelial electrical resistance only, never as a state estimate.

band        frequency     dominant element                 role in the fit
----------  ------------  -------------------------------  ---------------------------
very low    100 Hz        electrode interface (CPE)        interface magnitude and exponent
low         500 Hz        extracellular / barrier R        matrix-axis resistive band
low         1 kHz         extracellular / barrier R        matrix-axis resistive band
mid         4 kHz         barrier R with membrane C        transition
mid         10 kHz        membrane capacitance             cell-number-sensitive
high        40 kHz        coverage-dominated               coverage band
very high   100 kHz       series lead and interface        high-frequency roll-off

Sweep cadence 30 min, fitted to the equivalent circuit, geometric correction
applied from the printed device geometry and never refitted per device,
timepoint or arm. Waveform, amplitude and frequency set are fixed for the life
of the study and the residual excitation-shape offset is carried as a bounded
systematic rather than corrected away.
